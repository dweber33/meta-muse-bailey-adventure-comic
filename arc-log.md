# Bailey Arc Log

The full story, day by day. Each day is one connected arc across three strips: morning (6am) → midday (12pm) → evening (6pm), sent to Karen on Messenger and posted as one carousel on Instagram at 6pm. The story bible keeps only the last 7 days; everything lives on here.

Each day opens with a tag line — *shape · settings · cast · ending* — so patterns are easy to spot.

## Thu Nov 12, 2026 — The Beginning
*Two stand-alone strips · the bed, the house · the Squirrel (in a dream) · treats recovered*
- **Strip 1, "Professional Napper":** Bailey naps on her green checkered bed and dreams of a squirrel with an acorn.
- **Strip 2, "Bailey, Detective":** Bailey solves the case of the missing treat jar; treats recovered.
- First strips ever. Bailey is established: small black-and-tan scruffy female dog, big pointy ears. The Squirrel is her dream rival. Titles: Professional Napper, Detective.

## Sat Nov 14, 2026 — Super Bailey
*Daydream → it comes true → patrol · blanket fort, front yard and big oak · Mittens (new), Daniel · asleep in bed, dreaming of flying*
- **Morning, "Bailey and the Blanket Fort":** a chilly 51°F pre-dawn. Bailey wakes cold, builds a blanket fort on her bed, crowns herself Queen of the Fort (sock crown), and tells Daniel, "No cold mornings allowed in my fort!" She daydreams of being a superhero in a red blanket-cape.
- **Midday, "Super Bailey" (Daniel's pick):** in her red blanket-cape in the breezy front yard (63°F, autumn leaves swirling), Bailey hears a tiny "mew!" and rescues Mittens, the neighbor's orange tabby kitten, from the big oak, catching her in the cape. Title earned: Super Bailey, Hero of the Yard.
- **Evening, "Super Bailey's Night Patrol":** one last hero patrol on a clear 58°F evening — the Squirrel asleep in the oak with her acorn, Mittens waving goodnight from the neighbor's fence. Daniel lets her in; she hangs up the cape, curls into bed with a treat, and dreams of flying over the yard. New routine: the Night Patrol. The debut Instagram carousel went up (later deleted and reposted with a fixed panel 3: Bailey's and Mittens's lines had been swapped).

## Mon Nov 16, 2026 — The Gator & The Swamp Patrol
*Plan → patrol → storm · the bed, the yard and big oak · Toy Gator, the Squirrel, Daniel · thunder ritual; asleep in bed*
- **Morning, "Bailey & the Gator — Guard Duty at Dawn":** a cool 58°F dawn. The Toy Gator comes alive in Bailey's imagination, wakes her at 6am, and teaches her a swamp growl ("Grrr... arooo!"). By sunrise he's a plush again, but Bailey knows the truth. She tucks Ruby's biscuit into her blanket and declares a Swamp Patrol.
- **Midday, "Bailey & the Gator — The Swamp Patrol":** a breezy 78°F noon. The patrol: the biscuit left at the foot of the oak by Ruby's nest (Ruby is out — no meetup, just the delivery), the Squirrel chased up the oak (still uncaught), the yard declared secure, and a nap in the oak's shade.
- **Evening, "Bailey & the Gator — A Rainy Night":** rain arrives at dusk (66°F). Bailey dashes inside carrying the Gator, Daniel lets her in with a towel, and a thunder rumble brings out the thunder ritual for the first time — Bailey and Daniel bark at the thunder together at the window ("Arooo!"). She falls asleep with the Gator beside her.

## Wed Nov 18, 2026 — The Squirrel's Truce
*Rival → truce → delivery · windowsill, big oak in the rain · the Squirrel, Daniel, Karen · thunder ritual; asleep in bed, dreaming*
- **Morning, "Bailey & the Squirrel — A Truce at Dawn":** a cloudy 64°F dawn. The Squirrel leaves an acorn on the windowsill as a peace offering and waves from the big oak — "Truce?" Bailey says yes and takes one biscuit from the treat jar to give her in return. Dream rival turned friend.
- **Midday, "Bailey & the Squirrel — A Biscuit in the Rain":** a rainy 73°F noon. Bailey carries the truce biscuit to the big oak; the Squirrel greets her ("You came!"). Bailey sets it at the foot of the tree ("A truce biscuit — for you!"), they shelter together under the branches as the rain falls harder, and Bailey trots home wet and happy ("Best truce ever!").
- **Evening, "Bailey & the Squirrel — A Truce Night":** a rainy 68°F evening. A towel-off at the door, the thunder ritual with Daniel, and the Squirrel waving goodnight from the oak ("Goodnight, friend!"). Karen tucks her in with the peace-offering acorn on the windowsill and the Gator beside her; she dreams of sharing acorns under a starry oak.

## Fri Nov 20, 2026 — Holly the Hummingbird
*New friend: meet → garden → cliffhanger · bedroom window, garden · Holly (new) · ends on a cliffhanger — someone's digging in Holly's garden*
- **Morning, "Bailey & the Hummingbird — A Dawn Visitor":** a cloudy 70°F dawn under a flood watch. Bailey meets Holly, a tiny emerald-green hummingbird with a pink throat who zips by every dawn. Too fast to chat, Holly leaves a tiny pink flower on the windowsill and invites Bailey to the garden flowers after breakfast. Bailey curls back into bed, the flower beside her pillow.
- **Midday, "Bailey & Holly — A Garden Hello":** a cloudy 74°F noon. Bailey carries the flower to the garden, spins in circles trying to follow Holly's darting flight, sneezes into a big blossom, and gives Holly the tiny pink flower ("For me? Thank you!"). It's Holly's now. They end lying together among the flowers ("Best garden day ever!").
- **Evening, "The Garden Digger — Holes in the Dark":** a cloudy 72°F night after a 5:31pm sunset. On Night Patrol in Holly's garden, Bailey finds fresh little holes dug among the pink flowers and Holly's tiny pink flower knocked into the dirt; she sets it back up. Holly is fast asleep on her twig and can't say who did it. A rustle at the fence — two glowing eyes glint in the dark — then gone. "Someone's digging in Holly's garden. Detective Bailey is on the case!" Ends on a cliffhanger: "To be continued…"
- **Story note:** Daniel flagged that the adventures were getting predictable — wake up, meet someone new, do something together, go home. Three levers went into the bible: vary the shape, bring back old friends first, raise the stakes. The same day, the bible was restructured: the story history lives here, production notes moved to `production-log.md`, and the bible gained Today's plan, Open threads, Storytelling craft, and Story seeds.

## Sat Nov 21, 2026 — The Garden Digger
- **Morning, "The Garden Digger — Clues at Dawn":** a cloudy 70°F dawn, sunrise 6:53 a.m., showers coming later. Bailey lies wide awake in bed, thinking about the case — the Toy Gator, her partner, beside her. At first light she pads out to Holly's garden and finds two clues: an acorn cap in one of the holes ("The Squirrel's been here") and a tuft of orange fur on the fence ("Mittens?"). Two suspects, one garden, case open.
- **Midday, "The Garden Digger — A Shaky Truce":** a partly cloudy 76°F noon. Mittens confesses the glinting eyes were hers — she was chasing a moth — and begs to be Bailey's sidekick. The acorn cap leads them to the big oak, where the Squirrel has dirt on her nose. Bailey accuses her ("You dug up Holly's garden!") and the Squirrel, hurt, says "I thought we had a truce!" and dashes up the oak. The truce is in trouble.
- **Evening, "The Garden Digger — The Truth at Dusk":** a rainy 73°F dusk, thunderstorms coming later tonight. Bailey carries a peace biscuit to the big oak with Mittens at her side. The Squirrel climbs down, dirt on her nose, and confesses she never knew the garden was Holly's home — she was only burying acorns for winter. Together the three move the whole stash from the garden holes to the roots of the big oak, and Holly sleeps safe on her twig in the rain ("Sleep tight, Holly. Your garden is safe."). Button: inspired, Bailey buries a biscuit of her own in the garden — "For winter. Just in case." The truce survives its first test, and Mittens is officially Bailey's sidekick.
*Mystery, day 2 of a two-day arc · garden, big oak · the Squirrel, Mittens, Holly · ends with Bailey burying her own biscuit in Holly's garden — not asleep in bed*

## Sun Nov 22, 2026 — Daisy's Been Waiting
- **Morning, "Daisy's Been Waiting — A Promise to Keep":** a cloudy 65°F dawn, sunrise 6:53 a.m. Bailey dreams of splashing with Daisy at the pond — then wakes with a jolt: she promised Daisy she'd come back "tomorrow," nine sunrises ago. She packs for the pond like an expedition (biscuit, bone, Gator), tells Karen ("Today's the day, Karen! The pond!"), and heads out the front door with Daniel holding the leash ("Daisy, I'm coming!").
- **Midday, "Daisy's Been Waiting — A Splashy Apology":** a partly cloudy 75°F noon, breezy. Partly Daisy's point of view: Daisy floats alone on a lily pad, counting — "Sunrise number nine... still no Bailey." Bailey arrives at the pond with Daniel holding the leash, biscuit in her mouth ("Daisy! I'm here!"), but Daisy turns her back — "Nine mornings I waited, Bailey." Bailey sets her best biscuit down at the water's edge ("I'm sorry, Daisy"), then leaps in with a big SPLASH among the cattails. Daisy laughs, takes the biscuit, and forgives her — "One splash race — then all is forgiven!"
- **Evening, "Daisy's Been Waiting — Stars by the Pond":** a mostly cloudy 69°F night, sunset 5:30 p.m., waxing gibbous moon. Bailey and Daniel carry the plaid blanket to the pond at dusk; Daisy greets them from her lily pad ("Quack! You came back!"). They spread the blanket on the bank and count stars ("One... two... three... a hundred!"). Daisy spots a shooting star ("Look — a shooting star!") and Bailey wishes for more pond days. Sleepy-eyed, Bailey curls up on the blanket as Daisy tucks in on her lily pad, and Daniel drapes the red blanket-cape over her: "Promise kept, Bailey." The Nov 13 dream come true — the promise kept.
*Point of view, making amends · park pond · Daisy, Daniel · ends asleep under the stars at the pond — promise kept*
