# Bailey Arc Log

A day-by-day log of Bailey's adventures. Each day is one connected arc across
three strips: morning (6am) → midday (12pm) → evening (6pm), sent to Karen
on Messenger and posted as one carousel on Instagram at 6pm.

## Nov 12, 2026 — The Beginning

- **Strip 1, "Professional Napper":** Bailey naps on her green checkered bed
  and dreams of a squirrel with an acorn.
- **Strip 2, "Bailey, Detective":** Bailey solves the case of the missing
  treat jar.
- First strips ever. Bailey is established: small black-and-tan scruffy dog,
  big pointy ears, she/her. The Squirrel is her dream rival.

## Nov 13, 2026 — The Vacuum & The Park

- **Morning:** "Bailey vs. the vacuum" — Bailey barks the Vacuum Monster into
  submission and stands on it victorious. Title earned: Vacuum Defender.
- **Midday:** "Park Adventures • Day 1" — butterflies and the pond; Bailey
  meets Daisy the Duck (small green-and-white duck, named by Daniel).
- **Evening:** "Bailey dreams of a starry pond" — home tired with muddy paws,
  dreaming of counting stars with Daisy.
- The three-a-day schedule for Karen begins. The main-chat strip is retired.

## Nov 14, 2026 — Super Bailey

- **Morning, "Bailey and the Blanket Fort":** Bailey wakes cold, builds a
  blanket fort, crowns herself Queen of the Fort, and daydreams of being a
  superhero in a red blanket-cape.
- **Midday, "Super Bailey" (Daniel's pick):** In her red blanket-cape, Bailey
  rescues Mittens the orange tabby kitten from the front-yard oak tree,
  catching her in the cape. Title earned: Super Bailey, Hero of the Yard.
- **Evening, "Super Bailey's Night Patrol":** One last hero patrol — the
  squirrel asleep in the oak, Mittens waving goodnight — then cape hung up,
  bed, and dreams of flying. The debut Instagram carousel goes up (later
  deleted and reposted with a fixed panel 3: Bailey's and Mittens's lines had
  been swapped).
- New routine: the Night Patrol.

## Nov 15, 2026 — Ruby the Cardinal

- **Morning, "Bailey & Ruby — A Chilly Morning in Kissimmee":** Bailey hears
  singing at the window and meets Ruby the Cardinal; a dawn duet and a
  sunrise over the golf course. Ruby's nest is in the big oak tree.
- **Midday, "Ruby's Midday Song" (Daniel's pick):** Bailey visits Ruby's nest,
  follows her song to a sunny spot on the golf course, and they sing together.
  Ruby's farewell — "Same time tomorrow, Bailey!" — is a friendly goodbye,
  not a standing meetup.
- **Evening, "A Treat for Tomorrow":** Bailey picks one biscuit from the
  treat jar for Ruby tomorrow, stargazes from the porch, and falls asleep to
  a bedtime story. (A bubble-tail error in the "Tucked In" panel — Karen's
  "Good night, sleepy girl." read as Bailey talking to herself — was left as
  is at Daniel's call. The pre-posting check was tightened the same night to
  audit every bubble's tail, one by one.)

## Nov 16, 2026 — The Gator & The Swamp Patrol

- **Morning, "Bailey & the Gator — Guard Duty at Dawn":** The Toy Gator comes
  alive in Bailey's imagination and teaches her a swamp growl; they plan a
  Swamp Patrol of the yard.
- **Midday, "Bailey & the Gator — The Swamp Patrol":** The patrol — biscuit
  delivered to Ruby's nest, the Squirrel chased (still uncaught), yard
  declared secure, nap in the oak's shade.
- **Evening, "Bailey & the Gator — A Rainy Night":** Rain arrives; Bailey
  dashes inside with the Gator, and the thunder ritual makes its first
  appearance — Bailey and Daniel bark at the thunder together at the window.

## Nov 17, 2026 — Finn the Frog

- **Morning, "Bailey & the Frog — A Rainy Morning":** Bailey meets Finn the
  Frog at the rainy bedroom window; they plan a puddle day. (The strip was
  briefly held over a 55°/65° temperature mix-up; Daniel asked "Why can't you
  just fix it?" — the fix rule changed that day: keep fixing until it passes,
  never hold a strip.)
- **Midday, "Bailey & Finn — A Puddle Day":** Splashing through yard puddles
  — Finn's big hop, a puddle race, both muddy and happy as Puddle Champions.
- **Evening, "Bailey & Finn — A Muddy Bath & Goodnight":** Home soaking wet
  and muddy; a bubble bath, a bedtime story, Karen tucks her in.

## Nov 18, 2026 — The Squirrel's Truce

- **Morning, "Bailey & the Squirrel — A Truce at Dawn":** The Squirrel leaves
  an acorn on the windowsill as a peace offering — "Truce?" Bailey says yes
  and takes a biscuit to leave for her in return. Dream rival turned friend.
- **Midday, "Bailey & the Squirrel — A Biscuit in the Rain":** Bailey delivers
  the promised truce biscuit to the Squirrel at the big oak in the light rain;
  they shelter together under the branches.
- **Evening, "Bailey & the Squirrel — A Truce Night":** Home wet, towel-off,
  the thunder ritual with Daniel, the Squirrel waving goodnight from the oak.
  Bailey falls asleep with the peace-offering acorn on the windowsill,
  dreaming of sharing acorns under a starry oak.

## Nov 19, 2026 — Official Thanksgiving Taste Tester

- **Morning, "Bailey & the Thanksgiving Taste Test":** Bailey wakes to the
  smell of pumpkin pie, follows it to the kitchen, and Daniel gives her one
  tiny taste of crust as the Official Thanksgiving Taste Tester. Thanksgiving
  is one week away. (Daniel's noon guidance: no table-setting strip — "it's
  not Thanksgiving just yet" — but anything else is fine, surprise him.)
- **Midday, "Bailey & the Taste Test Report":** Bailey reports her pie verdict
  to Glenda and Brad at their house, passes Grandpa Brad's cranberry-sauce
  second test, and is declared "Officially approved — by Grandma and
  Grandpa!" First strip set at the grandparents' house.
- **Evening, "Bailey & the Thanksgiving Menu":** Home wet from the
  grandparents'; Bailey reports to Karen, and they plan the Thanksgiving menu
  together — pie first on the list — Bailey stamps her paw print on the menu
  card ("Officially approved!") and falls asleep dreaming of the feast.
- New: the paw-print menu stamp as Bailey's official seal of approval.

## Nov 20, 2026 — Holly the Hummingbird

- **Morning, "Bailey & the Hummingbird — A Dawn Visitor":** Bailey meets
  Holly, a tiny emerald-green hummingbird with a pink throat, at the bedroom
  window at dawn. Holly leaves a tiny pink flower on the windowsill and
  invites Bailey to the garden flowers after breakfast.
- **Midday, "Bailey & Holly — A Garden Hello":** Bailey meets Holly among the
  garden flowers, spins trying to follow her darting flight, sneezes into a
  blossom, and gives Holly the tiny pink flower.
- **Evening:** to come.
- **Story note (Nov 20):** Daniel flagged the adventures were getting
  predictable — wake up, meet someone new, do something together, go home.
  Three levers were added to the bible: vary the shape, bring back old
  friends first, raise the stakes. The evening run is the first under the
  new rules.
