# Bailey Characters

Full character sheets for Bailey's world: how everyone looks, who they are, how they talk, what's happened so far, and where their stories could go. The story bible carries the short version the Muse reads every run, plus each character's Last on-page date. Keep the two in step.

Real people and real pets come first: their looks and personalities are Daniel's call.

## Bailey

*Personality, voice, wants, and flaws are drawn from the strips so far. Daniel, add the real Bailey's quirks.*

- **Look:** small black-and-tan scruffy female dog. Short scruffy fur (not long or shaggy). Big pointy ears that never flop, whether she's awake, asleep, wet, or curled up.
- **Pronouns:** she/her/hers, natural pronouns only.
- **Home base:** the green checkered bed with a white pillow.
- **Backstory:** adopted from Daniel's grandparents on his dad's side. When they passed away, it was their wish she stay in the family.
- **Loves:** naps, treats, chasing butterflies, the park pond, playing on the golf course.
- **Personality:** big heart, small dog. She takes her jobs and titles very seriously, and the comedy lives in the gap between her confidence and her size. Food-motivated, a champion napper, and a loyal guardian of her bed, her yard, her family, and her friends. Thunder scares her, but with Daniel beside her she's brave.
- **Voice:** short, earnest declarations — proud, a little dramatic, never mean. "No cold mornings allowed in my fort!"
- **Wants:** to be useful, to be a hero, and to get the treat.
- **Flaws that make stories:** overconfidence (she's tiny but acts big), impatience, and a weakness for treats and naps at the worst moments. Dog logic: she misreads human things — the vacuum is a monster, the garbage truck eats cans.
- **Titles collected:** Professional Napper (Nov 12), Detective (Nov 12), Vacuum Defender (Nov 13), Queen of the Fort (Nov 14), Super Bailey / Hero of the Yard (Nov 14), Official Thanksgiving Taste Tester (Nov 19).
- **Rituals and routines:**
  - **The thunder ritual:** when it thunders, Bailey gets scared and comes to Daniel; they bark at the thunder together, which makes her feel brave and protected. She only barks at the thunder when Daniel is with her.
  - **The Night Patrol** (Nov 14): one last round of the front yard before bed.
  - **The Swamp Patrol** (Nov 16): a yard patrol with the Toy Gator.
  - **The paw-print stamp** (Nov 19): her official seal of approval.

## Bailey's humans

They appear only when the story calls for them, and Bailey is always the focus. They're real people: keep them kind and true to life.

### Daniel
- **Look:** dark hair, short beard, usually a black shirt.
- **Who he is to Bailey:** her owner and her thunder buddy — the one she runs to when Lightning flashes. He hands out the taste tests and the biscuits, reads bedtime stories, and lets her in from her patrols.
- **On-page so far:** peeks into the blanket fort with his mug (Nov 14); bedtime stories (Nov 15, Nov 17); the thunder ritual (Nov 16, Nov 18); the first Thanksgiving taste of crust (Nov 19).

### Karen Tijerina-Weber
- **Look:** long dark hair, often a black dress with white polka dots.
- **Who she is to Bailey:** her owner, Daniel's wife, and the strip's first reader every day. She tucks Bailey in ("Good night, sleepy girl."), baked the Thanksgiving pumpkin pie, and planned the menu with her.
- **On-page so far:** tucks Bailey in (Nov 15, Nov 17, Nov 18); cools the pumpkin pie and plans the menu (Nov 19).
- **Story potential:** real moments with Bailey beyond bedtime. Daniel knows the best ones.

### Glenda (Grandma)
- **Look:** reddish-blonde hair, glasses, often a magenta/burgundy coat.
- **Who she is to Bailey:** Daniel's mom, Bailey's grandma. She and Brad live in a different house from Daniel and Karen; Bailey visits them there.
- **On-page so far:** heard Bailey's pie report and declared her "Officially approved — by Grandma and Grandpa!" (Nov 19).

### Brad (Grandpa)

- **Who he is to Bailey:** Daniel's dad, Bailey's grandpa.
- **On-page so far:** gave her the cranberry-sauce second test (Nov 19).

### Jennifer Ruben (Aunt Jennifer)

- **Who she is to Bailey:** Daniel's sister, Bailey's aunt. She and Andrew live in a different house on the golf course; Bailey visits them there.

### Andrew Ruben (Uncle Andrew)

## Friends

### The Squirrel
- **Look:** a squirrel who always has her acorn. Keep her look from earlier strips.
- **Personality:** quick, clever, always stashing acorns for later. Proud, a little prickly, and new to being friends.
- **Voice:** fast chatter.
- **With Bailey:** dream rival turned friend. The truce is new: it can be tested, and it can grow.
- **History:** Bailey dreamed of catching her and her acorn in the very first strip (Nov 12) and chased her through the yard on Nov 16 (still uncaught). On Nov 18 she left an acorn on the windowsill and asked for a truce; Bailey said yes and delivered a biscuit to her at the big oak in the rain. That night she waved goodnight from the oak.
- **Home:** the big oak in the front yard.
- **Name:** unknown. A story could reveal it, or Daniel can name her.
- **Story potential:** a day told from her point of view; her winter acorn stash; finally learning her name.

### Daisy the Duck
- **Look:** small green-and-white duck (not a mallard).
- **Personality:** playful, splashy, giggly, and loyal.
- **Voice:** cheerful quacks and short, bubbly lines.
- **With Bailey:** her first animal friend.
- **History:** met Nov 13 at the park pond ("Park Adventures • Day 1"); they splashed and quacked together. Named by Daniel that day, she keeps her name and look in every strip. That night Bailey dreamed of counting stars by the pond with her, and of "tomorrow's visit." That visit never happened.
- **Home:** the park pond, among the lily pads and cattails.
- **Story potential:** Park Adventures • Day 2. Bailey owes her a visit.
- **Care note:** nobody feeds Daisy bread.

### Mittens the Kitten
- **Look:** small orange tabby kitten.
- **Personality:** tiny, curious, and fearless until she's stuck.
- **Voice:** little "mew!"s and small, eager lines.
- **With Bailey:** as far as Mittens is concerned, Bailey saved her life. She adores Super Bailey.
- **History:** stuck in the big oak in the front yard on Nov 14, she jumped into Bailey's red blanket-cape like a safety net and thanked her. That night she waved goodnight from the neighbor's fence.
- **Home:** next door. She's the neighbor's cat.
- **Story potential:** Super Bailey's sidekick; a storm night where Bailey brings her to the thunder ritual.

### Ruby the Cardinal
- **Look:** bright red cardinal with a crest.
- **Personality:** a cheerful early bird, proud of her singing and of the nest she built herself.
- **Voice:** musical, sing-song lines.
- **With Bailey:** her dawn-duet partner.
- **History:** sang at Bailey's window at dawn on Nov 15 — a duet and a sunrise over the golf course. At noon Bailey visited her nest, and they sang together in a sunny spot on the golf course. Her "Same time tomorrow, Bailey!" was a friendly farewell, not a standing daily meetup (Daniel's call, Nov 15). Bailey left her the promised biscuit at the foot of the oak on Nov 16 while she was out.
- **Home:** her nest in the big oak in the front yard.
- **Story potential:** thanking Bailey for that biscuit; the bird expert in "The Mystery Egg."

### Finn the Frog
- **Look:** small bright green tree frog.
- **Personality:** bouncy, boastful about his big hop, competitive. Loves rain.
- **Voice:** quick brags and ribbits.
- **With Bailey:** her fellow Puddle Champion.
- **History:** met Nov 17 at the rainy bedroom window. That noon they splashed through the yard puddles — his big hop, a puddle race — and ended muddy and happy as Puddle Champions. That evening he waved goodnight from the doorstep: "Goodnight, Puddle Champion!"
- **Home:** the yard, near the puddles.
- **Story potential:** a puddle rematch on the next rainy day.

### Holly the Hummingbird
- **Look:** tiny emerald-green hummingbird with a pink throat and blur-fast wings.
- **Personality:** zippy, generous, can't stay still. At night she sleeps deeply, perched on a twig (a real hummingbird habit).
- **Voice:** quick, breathless bursts.
- **With Bailey:** a brand-new friend.
- **History:** met Nov 20 at the bedroom window at dawn; she zips by every dawn, and she left a tiny pink flower on the windowsill as a hello. At noon in the garden, Bailey spun in circles trying to follow her, sneezed into a blossom, and gave her the tiny pink flower. It's Holly's now.
- **Home:** the garden, among the flowers.
- **Story potential:** "The Garden Digger" (Nov 20–21); "The Mystery Egg" ("Mine are the size of jellybeans!").

### George Michael
- **Look:** black-and-white tuxedo cat. Black back and head; white chest, belly, and paws; white blaze on his forehead; big pointy ears. Reference photo on file.
- **Personality:** a real cat, so Daniel will confirm. Until then: a cool, dignified cousin who's hard to impress.
- **With Bailey:** her cousin.
- **Home:** Jennifer and Andrew's house on the golf course.
- **History:** not yet on-page. He appears only when the story calls for him.
- **Story potential:** Bailey's first visit to the golf-course house.

## Antagonists — the troublemakers of Bailey's world

### The Vacuum (Vacuum Monster)
- The roaring monster from the closet that sucks up crumbs and treats.
- **History:** Bailey barked it into submission and stood on top of it in victory on Nov 13, earning the title Vacuum Defender. Defeated for now, but it lurks in the closet and wants a rematch.
- **Story potential:** Thanksgiving crumbs, the perfect stage for the rematch.

### The Garbage Truck
- A big growling truck that rumbles down the street on garbage day, beeping as it backs up and gobbling up the cans. Loud, smelly, and far too big to bark into submission. Bailey guards the yard and outlasts it.
- **History:** not yet on-page.
- **Story potential:** a race to rescue a toy from the bin before the truck arrives.

### Lightning
- The flash in the storm clouds, crackling and teasing, with Thunder as its roaring voice. It scares Bailey, but when it flashes she runs to Daniel and they bark at the thunder together. It's the one foe she faces with Daniel at her side.
- **History:** the thunder ritual on Nov 16 and Nov 18.

## Bailey's toys

### The Toy Gator
- **Look:** green-and-blue plush alligator with yellow spikes along his back.
- **In Bailey's imagination:** he comes alive as a gruff, loyal swamp buddy who guards the bed, and he's a plush again by the end of the scene.
- **History:** on Nov 16 he woke her at 6am, taught her a swamp growl ("Grrr... arooo!"), and became her partner for the Swamp Patrol of the yard. He's usually beside her in bed.

### The Irish Bone ("KISS ME" bone)
- **Look:** black bone-shaped toy covered in green and yellow shamrocks, with "KISS ME" stitched across it.
- **What it is:** Bailey's favorite chew toy. She carries it around and guards it proudly.
- **Drawing rule:** the bone itself only — a bone shape lying down or being carried, never a character with a face, arms, or a body.
- **Story potential:** it has never been the center of a story, which makes it the best candidate for a lost-toy day.
