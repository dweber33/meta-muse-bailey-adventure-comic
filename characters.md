# Bailey Characters

## Bailey

- Small black-and-tan scruffy female dog, big pointy ears that never flop,
  short scruffy fur (not long or shaggy). She/her, natural pronouns only.
- Home base: the green checkered bed with a white pillow.
- Backstory: adopted from Daniel's grandparents on his dad's side. When they
  passed away, it was their wish she stay in the family.
- Loves: naps, treats, chasing butterflies, the park pond, playing on the
  golf course.
- Titles collected: Professional Napper, Detective, Vacuum Defender, Queen of
  the Fort, Super Bailey / Hero of the Yard, Official Thanksgiving Taste
  Tester.
- Thunder ritual: when it thunders, Bailey gets scared and comes to Daniel;
  they bark at the thunder together, which makes her feel brave and
  protected. She only barks at the thunder when Daniel is with her.

## Bailey's humans

- **Daniel** — Bailey's owner. Dark hair, short beard, usually a black shirt.
- **Karen Tijerina-Weber** — Bailey's owner, Daniel's wife. Long dark hair,
  often a black dress with white polka dots.
- **Glenda** — Daniel's mom, Bailey's grandma. Reddish-blonde hair, glasses,
  often a magenta/burgundy coat.
- **Brad** — Daniel's dad, Bailey's grandpa. Short dark hair, mustache, often
  a blue polo shirt.
- Glenda and Brad live in a different house from Daniel and Karen; Bailey
  visits them there.
- **Jennifer Ruben** — Daniel's sister, Bailey's aunt. Long wavy light-brown
  hair, often a white floral dress.
- **Andrew Ruben** — Jennifer's husband, Bailey's uncle. Short dark hair,
  full dark beard, often a tan long-sleeve shirt.
- Jennifer and Andrew live in a different house on the golf course; Bailey
  visits them there.
- Humans appear only when the story calls for them. Bailey is always the
  focus.

## Friends

- **The Squirrel** (with an acorn) — Bailey's dream rival since the first
  strip (Nov 12): Bailey dreamed of catching her and her acorn. On Nov 18
  she asked for a truce, leaving an acorn on the windowsill as a peace
  offering; Bailey answered yes with a biscuit. Dream rival turned friend.
- **Daisy the Duck** — small green-and-white duck (not a mallard). Met Nov 13
  at the park pond; they splashed and quacked together. Named Daisy by
  Daniel.
- **Mittens the Kitten** — small orange tabby, the neighbor's cat. Bailey
  rescued her from the front-yard oak tree Nov 14, catching her in the red
  blanket-cape. Lives next door.
- **Ruby the Cardinal** — bright red cardinal with a crest who sings at
  Bailey's window at dawn. Met Nov 15; a dawn duet and a sunrise over the
  golf course. Her nest is in the big oak tree in the front yard.
- **Finn the Frog** — small bright green tree frog. Met Nov 17 at the rainy
  bedroom window; they had a puddle day in the yard and ended muddy and
  happy as Puddle Champions. Lives in the yard, near the puddles.
- **Holly the Hummingbird** — tiny emerald-green hummingbird with a pink
  throat and blur-fast wings. Met Nov 20 at the bedroom window at dawn; she
  zips by every dawn and left a tiny pink flower on the windowsill. Lives in
  the garden, among the flowers.
- **George Michael** — Jennifer and Andrew's black-and-white tuxedo cat,
  Bailey's cousin. Black back and head, white chest, belly and paws, white
  blaze on his forehead, big pointy ears. Lives at Jennifer and Andrew's
  house on the golf course.

## Antagonists

- **The Vacuum (Vacuum Monster)** — the roaring monster from the closet that
  sucks up crumbs and treats. Bailey barked it into submission Nov 13 and
  stood on it victorious. Defeated for now, but it lurks in the closet and
  may roar back for a rematch.
- **The Garbage Truck** — a big growling truck that rumbles down the street
  on garbage day, beeping as it backs up and gobbling up the cans. Bailey
  guards the yard from it — loud, smelly, far too big to bark into
  submission.
- **Lightning** — the flash in the storm clouds, crackling and teasing, with
  Thunder as its roaring voice. It scares Bailey, but per the thunder ritual
  she runs to Daniel and they bark at the thunder together. The one foe she
  faces with Daniel at her side.

## Bailey's toys

- **The Toy Gator** — green-and-blue plush alligator with yellow spikes along
  its back. In Bailey's imagination he can come alive as a friendly swamp
  buddy who guards the bed. He did exactly that Nov 16: guard-duty lessons
  at dawn and the Swamp Patrol of the yard.
- **The Irish Bone ("KISS ME" bone)** — black bone-shaped toy covered in
  green and yellow shamrocks, "KISS ME" stitched across it. Bailey's favorite
  chew toy. Draw it as the bone itself only — a bone shape lying down or
  being carried, never as a character with a face, arms, or a body.

## Places

- The green checkered bed (home).
- The park: flower meadow with butterflies, pond with lily pads and
  cattails, where Daisy lives.
- Glenda and Brad's house — Daniel's parents' home.
- Jennifer and Andrew's house on the golf course.
- The golf course — Bailey's playground in their golf course community.
- The front yard and the big oak tree — Ruby's nest, Mittens's rescue,
  the Squirrel's home.
